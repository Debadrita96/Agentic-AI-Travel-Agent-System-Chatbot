# Bali Trip Guide

Bali is popular for mixed relaxation and activity trips.

Useful base areas:
- Ubud for greenery, temples, cafes, wellness
- Seminyak / Canggu for beach clubs and dining
- Nusa Dua for a calmer resort-oriented stay

Typical activities:
- temple visits
- waterfall trips
- beach sunsets
- rice terrace visits
- spa / wellness

Practical planning notes:
- travel times can be longer than expected
- choosing one main base can reduce wasted transit time
- midrange travelers often balance a comfortable stay with paid day trips
