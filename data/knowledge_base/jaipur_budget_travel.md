# Jaipur Budget Travel Notes

Jaipur works well for a budget-friendly cultural weekend.

Popular budget-friendly experiences:
- Hawa Mahal exterior and old city walks
- Amer Fort
- City Palace area
- local markets and street food zones

Budget notes:
- short stays can be efficient if attractions are grouped by area
- budget and midrange hotel options are often available
- local transport costs are usually manageable for short trips

A practical weekend plan:
- Day 1: old city + Hawa Mahal + market exploration
- Day 2: Amer Fort + cultural stop + local food
