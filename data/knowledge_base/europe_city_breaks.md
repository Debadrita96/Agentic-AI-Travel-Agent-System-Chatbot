# Europe City Breaks

For foodie-friendly and relatively budget-conscious city breaks, common candidates include:
- Lisbon
- Budapest
- Seville

Selection logic:
- Lisbon: food, views, walkable neighborhoods, strong city-break appeal
- Budapest: thermal baths, architecture, nightlife, broad mid-budget appeal
- Seville: culture, tapas, compact center, strong atmosphere

Budget-sensitive travelers may prefer:
- shoulder season travel
- central but not premium accommodation
- short stays with mostly walking-based itineraries
