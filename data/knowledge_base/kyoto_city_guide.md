# Kyoto City Guide

Kyoto is a strong choice for a short culture-focused trip.
Good areas to stay include:
- Gion for traditional atmosphere
- Kawaramachi for transport and food access
- Kyoto Station area for convenience

Popular experiences:
- Fushimi Inari early morning visit
- Kiyomizu-dera and Higashiyama walk
- Arashiyama bamboo grove and nearby temples
- Nishiki Market for food sampling

A practical 3-day rhythm:
- Day 1: Higashiyama, Gion, evening walk
- Day 2: Fushimi Inari, central Kyoto food and shopping
- Day 3: Arashiyama and a slower cultural stop

Travel style notes:
- Walking plus local rail works well
- Spring and autumn are attractive but busier
